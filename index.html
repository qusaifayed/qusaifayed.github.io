    Case study · Power BI

    Red Sea Crisis: Impact on Aqaba Port

    How the Red Sea shipping disruption changed vessel traffic and trade at Jordan's only seaport, comparing activity before and after the crisis.

    Data sourceIMF PortWatch · AIS vessel data
    Scope2019–2026 · 4 maritime chokepoints
    Tech stackPower BI · DAX
    AnalysisSupply-chain disruption & recovery
    aqaba_port_red_sea.pbix

    The question

    What did the crisis do to Aqaba?

    Attacks on shipping in the Red Sea pushed many carriers to avoid the Bab el-Mandeb route. Aqaba sits at the end of that route, so the question was how much its traffic and trade changed, and whether it has recovered.

    Approach

    1. 01Downloaded daily port activity for Aqaba from IMF PortWatch.
    2. 02Cleaned the data and built a date table for time comparisons.
    3. 03Wrote DAX measures for before/after averages and percentage change.
    4. 04Designed the report pages around the timeline of the crisis.

    Key findings

    What the data showed

    −19%

    Daily ship calls dropped from 3.11 to 2.53 when the Red Sea crisis hit.

    3.64

    Traffic recovered above the pre-crisis baseline: 3.50 calls a day in the lull and 3.64 during the regional war.

    32%

    Container ships are the largest share of calls since Nov 2023, ahead of dry bulk at 30%.

    Case study · Power BI

    Credit Card & Banking Dashboard

    Final project of the Power BI program at Qafza: a five-page report on a bank's card customers, cards, transactions and risk.

    ProgramData Analytics Track · Qafza
    Tech stackPower BI · DAX · Excel
    Dataset13.3M transactions · 6,146 cards
    Credit Card Analytics · Executive Overview

    The question

    Who are the card customers, how do they spend, and where is the risk?

    The report follows the business from the top down: an executive overview first, then customers, the card portfolio, transaction patterns, and finally risk and fraud.

    Approach

    1. 01Cleaned the customer, card and transaction data.
    2. 02Built the data model and relationships in Power BI.
    3. 03Wrote DAX measures for spending, credit limits and risk flags.
    4. 04Designed five report pages with side navigation, slicers and tooltips.

    Key findings

    What the data showed

    $571.8M

    Total spending across 13.31M transactions, an average of $42.98 per transaction.

    52%

    Mastercard holds 52% of the 6,146 cards issued, followed by Visa at 38%.

    282

    Customers flagged as high risk for high debt and low credit score, holding 55 high-risk cards.

    Pick a brand to see how many of the 6,146 issued cards it holds and its share of the portfolio, the same way the dashboard slicer filters the report.
